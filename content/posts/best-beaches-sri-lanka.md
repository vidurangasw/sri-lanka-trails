Sri Lanka's coastline stretches for **1,340 kilometres**, wrapping the island in an almost unbroken ribbon of golden sand, turquoise water, and swaying coconut palms. Whether you want to surf perfect barrels, snorkel with sea turtles, or simply disappear into a hammock with a fresh king coconut, Sri Lanka delivers.

Here are the beaches that genuinely deserve a place on your itinerary.

## 1. Mirissa — The Quintessential Paradise Beach

**Best for:** Whale watching, laid-back vibes, nightlife, Instagram

Located on the south coast, Mirissa is perhaps the most famous beach in Sri Lanka — and for good reason. The horseshoe-shaped bay is framed by coconut palms and offers calm, swimmable water from November to April.

What sets Mirissa apart is its position as the **world's best place for blue whale watching**. Between November and April, boats depart at dawn for a chance to see the largest animal ever to have lived on Earth.

![Crystal-clear turquoise water at Mirissa beach with traditional fishing outrigger boats in the foreground](/images/posts/beach.jpg)

**Best time to visit:** November to April (dry season, whale season)
**Getting there:** 3 hours from Colombo, or take the scenic coastal train to Matara (15 min away by tuk-tuk)

> **Pro Tip:** Skip the commercial whale-watching operators and book with a responsible, eco-certified operator. Look for operators who follow the Sri Lanka whale-watching code of conduct.

## 2. Arugam Bay — Sri Lanka's Surf Capital

**Best for:** Surfing (beginner to advanced), bohemian atmosphere, yoga

On the remote east coast, Arugam Bay is a legend in the global surfing community. The main break — **Main Point** — is considered one of the top 10 surf spots in the world, producing long, perfect right-handers that can reach 3 metres during peak season.

| Level | Best Spot |
|-------|-----------|
| Beginner | Pottuvil Point, Whisky Point |
| Intermediate | Whisky Point, Crocodile Rock |
| Advanced | Main Point |

**Best time to visit:** April to October (east coast dry season)
**Getting there:** 7 hours from Colombo; domestic flights available to Batticaloa (1.5 hours away)

## 3. Unawatuna — The Family Favourite

Just south of Galle, Unawatuna's protected bay creates naturally calm water ideal for families and swimmers. The snorkelling is excellent — you can spot reef fish, rays, and sea turtles without even renting a boat.

**Best time to visit:** November to April
**Don't miss:** Jungle Beach — a hidden cove accessible by a 15-minute jungle walk from Unawatuna

## 4. Nilaveli — The Unspoilt North

**Best for:** Complete solitude, pristine snorkelling, Pigeon Island National Park

If you want a Sri Lanka beach experience with almost no crowds, head to Nilaveli on the north-east coast. The water is impossibly clear, the sand is powder-white, and Pigeon Island — accessible by boat — offers some of the best snorkelling in the country, with blacktip reef sharks a common sight.

**Best time to visit:** May to September

## 5. Rekawa — Where Sea Turtles Nest

Rekawa Beach is not about sunbathing. It is about witnessing one of nature's most moving spectacles: **five species of sea turtles** coming ashore to nest, from April to September.

Guided night tours run by a local conservation NGO take you to see nesting turtles without disturbing them. All proceeds fund turtle protection programmes.

## Practical Information

### Getting Around the Coast
- **Trains:** The coastal railway from Colombo to Galle is one of the most scenic train journeys in Asia. Sit on the right-hand side heading south.
- **Tuk-tuks:** Excellent for short hops between beaches and towns. Negotiate fares in advance.
- **Private driver:** Best for multi-beach road trips. Expect to pay around USD $50–80 per day for a driver with a car.

### What to Pack
- High-SPF reef-safe sunscreen (coral protection)
- Rash guard for extended water time
- Sarong (required for temple visits near beaches)
- Small backpack for day trips
